# Good to know: Publish your app

## Preview and the published app

Preview runs your current work, only for you. The published app runs a saved
release, for everyone, at its public address. They are separate: changing
your project changes Preview at once and the published app only when you
publish again.

## When a publish fails

The Deploy page says which step failed and why. **View failure logs**, or the
failed release's **Logs** in **Releases**, show everything it did. Colleague
can read them with you and explain what went wrong. A failed publish leaves
the previous release online, if there was one. **Check build** runs the same
build steps as a publish, without publishing anything.

## This app is temporary

The practice app is removed when the lesson ends, so don't share its address
as if it were permanent. Your own projects publish the same way and stay
online until you take them offline or delete the project.
