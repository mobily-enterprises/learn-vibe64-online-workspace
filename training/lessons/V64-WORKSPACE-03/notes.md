# Good to know: Deploy your app

## Preview and the deployed app

Preview runs your current work, only for you. The deployed app runs a saved
release, for everyone, at its public address. They are separate: changing
your project changes Preview at once and the deployed app only when you
deploy again.

## When a deployment fails

Open the failed release's **Logs** in **Releases**. Colleague can read them
with you and explain what went wrong. A failed deployment leaves the
previous release online, if there was one.

## This app is temporary

The practice app is removed when the lesson ends, so don't share its address
as if it were permanent. Your own projects deploy the same way and stay
online until you take them offline or delete the project.
