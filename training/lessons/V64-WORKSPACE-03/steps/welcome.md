In this lesson you put an app on the internet for real. Your practice project
is a one-page sign for a small shop. You deploy it to a public address, open
it like any visitor would, change it and deploy again.

The practice app and its deployment belong to this lesson. When you finish,
restart or delete the lesson, the app is taken offline and removed.
