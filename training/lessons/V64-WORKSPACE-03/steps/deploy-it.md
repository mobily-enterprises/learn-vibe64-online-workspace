Press **Publish to staging**. Vibe64 shows where the app will go; choose
**Publish** to confirm. It then copies your saved work, starts the app and
checks that it answers. This takes a minute or two; the output shows each
stage.

When the **Publish to staging** card says **published**, press
**Check now**. If the publish fails, Deploy says why; open its logs from
**Releases**, or ask Colleague what went wrong.

```beats
show deploy-staging: Press Publish to staging, read where it goes, then press Publish.
check fact: Press Check now once the card says published.
```
