The owner's own AI subscription is for the owner. Team members use the
**team AI**: models the owner chooses for them in **AI Accounts**.

- Until the owner sets up team AI, members can't open projects; they see a
  message asking the owner to set it up.
- Members' Colleague and helpers use the team AI. Members can pick their own
  Colleague model from Colleague's menu.

```beats
do manage-accounts: Open AI Accounts.
show manage-accounts: Your AI connections and, for a team, the AI members use.
check done: Press Done when you know where the team AI is set up.
```
