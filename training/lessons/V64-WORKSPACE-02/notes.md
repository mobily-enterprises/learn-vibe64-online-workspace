# Good to know: People and team AI

## Accounts are sign-ins, not app users

The people in **Users** are the people who can sign in to this workspace.
Accounts inside the apps you build are separate and belong to each app.

## Passwords

Type passwords only into the protected form on **Users**. Never put a
password into a chat, a lesson or a project file.

## If you are a member

Some steps in this lesson are for the owner, so you see fewer of them. If you
can't open projects, ask the owner to set up team AI.
