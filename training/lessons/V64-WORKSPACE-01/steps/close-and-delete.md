Every project has a **Project actions** menu with **Close** and **Delete**.

- **Close** stops the project's Preview and work to free memory. Your work
  stays; open the project again to carry on.
- **Delete** keeps an archive of the project, then removes it. If the project
  has a deployed app, Delete also takes that app offline, stops it and frees
  its address so another project can use it. If the app can't be taken
  offline right now (for example while it is being deployed), Delete stops
  and you can try again.

A lesson's practice project goes the same way when you finish, restart or
delete the lesson.
