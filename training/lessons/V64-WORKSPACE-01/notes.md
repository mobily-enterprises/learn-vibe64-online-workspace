# Good to know: Your workspace

## Learn and Develop

**L** switches between Learn (lessons and their practice projects) and
Develop (your projects). Each keeps its own place, so switching back returns
you where you were.

## Deleted projects

Delete keeps an archive of the project's code and data before it removes the
project. A deployed app is taken offline as part of the deletion; its
production database is kept.

## Who can do what

Only the workspace owner adds projects and manages people. Everyone can take
lessons, which make their own practice projects.
