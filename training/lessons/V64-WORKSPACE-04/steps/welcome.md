A deployment takes a minute or two, and an app can fail while you are busy
with something else. In this lesson Colleague keeps an eye on your app for
you and tells you when something happens.

You deploy the same shop sign as in **Deploy your app**, in a new practice
project of its own. It is removed when this lesson ends.
