Colleague can watch a deployment for three things:

- **live**: the app is live at its address (said at once if it already is);
- **failed**: a deployment, or the running release, failed;
- **changed**: anything about the deployment changed, such as a new release
  or the app going offline.

Ask in plain words: “Tell me if the deployment fails”, “Tell me if anything
changes with my app”. Colleague checks with your own access, so it only
watches apps you can open.
