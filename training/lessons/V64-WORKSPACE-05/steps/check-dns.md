Choose **Check DNS** beside your domain. When Vibe64 finds the TXT record,
the domain shows **verified** and Vibe64 starts serving the app there, with
its own secure certificate. If it still says **pending**, wait a little and
check again.

Then open your domain in a browser: it shows the same sign as the Vibe64
address.

```beats
show deploy-address: Choose Check DNS beside your domain.
check done: Press Done when your domain shows the sign, or if you are reading along.
```
