Every deployed app has a Vibe64 address. You can also serve it at a domain of
your own, such as `shop.example.com`.

To do every step you need a domain you own and access to its DNS settings at
your domain provider. Without one, read along: the lesson explains each step,
and only the deployment and one question are required.

```beats
show lesson-bar: This lesson puts your practice app on a domain of your own.
```
